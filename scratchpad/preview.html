About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Miskatonic University, advised by Prof. Eleanor Vance. My research concerns how populations of neurons encode uncertainty, and what that computation implies for models of perceptual decision-making.

I work with large-scale electrophysiology and probabilistic models, and I'm currently interested in how attention reshapes population geometry over the course of a single trial.
