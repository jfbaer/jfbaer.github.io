A visual library of every **realizable Steenrod module** in a small range —
each picture is the module's cell diagram, drawn so the $\mathrm{Sq}^i$ operations
read cleanly. Colors follow the operation degree; the whole gallery follows the
site's light/dark theme. Click any card to copy its sseq JSON.
